PASTORAL PLANNER — INSTALL ON iPAD

The app is a set of files that must be opened once from a web address.
After that it runs fully offline and keeps all data on your iPad.

1. Put the folder online once (free, takes 2 minutes, from a computer):
   - Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page, OR
   - Upload the folder to a GitHub repository and turn on GitHub Pages.
   You will get a web address (https://...).

2. On the iPad, open that address in Safari.

3. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Open Pastoral Planner from the Home Screen.

4. From now on it works with no internet. Your planner, notes, drawings and PDFs
   are stored only on this iPad, not on the website.

KEEP YOUR DATA SAFE
- Use "Backup & settings" > "Back up to Files" regularly (e.g. weekly) and keep
  the backup in iCloud Drive or Files. "Restore from backup" brings it all back.
- Always open the Home Screen app, not a Safari tab: iPadOS can clear data for
  websites that are not added to the Home Screen.

APPLE CALENDAR
- Import: in Calendar on a Mac or iCloud.com, export/share a calendar as an .ics file,
  save it to Files, then use "Import .ics file".
- Export: "Export my events" or "Add to Apple Calendar" on any event creates an .ics
  file that iPadOS offers to add to Calendar.

WRITING
- Pen, highlighter, eraser, lasso, text box, stickers and images are in the dock.
- "Pencil only" on: only Apple Pencil writes; fingers scroll and tap.
- Handwriting to text: choose the arrow (Type & tap) tool, then write with Apple Pencil
  in any text field. iPadOS Scribble converts it on the device.
- Dictation: tap a field, then the microphone at the top or on the keyboard.
